EveryOS 0.1.41 refreshes Settings branding and navigation.

- Settings identifies its active section in the window title, such as “Settings - About”. The
  title follows section and language changes on desktop and mobile, and the duplicate heading
  above the settings content is removed.
- About uses the supplied EveryOS wordmark instead of the geometric mark and a separate product
  name. The wordmark follows the active light or dark theme, with compact spacing and a preserved
  aspect ratio on narrow windows.
- Browser tabs use a new transparent glass “e” favicon adapted from the supplied EveryOS artwork.
- The five standalone SDK packages advance together to 1.0.4 to keep generated projects and
  distribution links aligned with this core release. All 29 official applications are retained.
