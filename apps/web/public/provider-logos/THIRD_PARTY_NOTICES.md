# Remote access provider marks

These logos identify the corresponding integrations; they do not imply endorsement.
Tailscale and NetBird retain their respective trademarks. Assets are bundled locally, with
their original colors, geometry and aspect ratios, and are not fetched at runtime.

## Tailscale

Source: https://github.com/tailscale/tailscale/blob/main/client/web/src/assets/icons/tailscale-logo.svg

The nine-dot mark is extracted from the official horizontal lockup; only the wordmark and its
unused canvas are omitted. The original shapes, opacity and colors are unchanged.
The repository's BSD-3-Clause license is reproduced in `TAILSCALE-LICENSE.txt`.

## NetBird

Source: https://netbird.io/press/netbird-bird-only.svg

The official logomark is included unchanged from the [NetBird press kit](https://netbird.io/press).
Its original colors and proportions are preserved, without filters or added effects on the mark.
